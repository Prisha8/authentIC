How it works

Three steps from photo to verdict.

1 · Upload a photo

Drop in a picture of the chip with its package markings visible. That's all we need.

2 · AI inspects it

The part is identified and checked against its official datasheet: markings, pin count, package dimensions and surface condition.

3 · Get the verdict

A scored authenticity verdict with listed anomalies, annotated images and a downloadable PDF report.

Everything you need to vet a chip
